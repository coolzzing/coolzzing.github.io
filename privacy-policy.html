Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Overview

SIHYEON PARK respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you browse the website, submit an inquiry, subscribe to updates, or purchase artwork prints and print goods.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, inquiry details, product interests, order details, and shipping information. If you subscribe to email updates, we collect the email address you provide. Payment details are collected and processed by Stripe on its checkout pages; we do not receive your complete card number through our website.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, process and fulfill orders, provide order-related support, communicate about shipping or problems with a purchase, send updates you request, maintain records, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use visitor analytics or advertising trackers on our website.

Service Providers

We use Stripe to process payments and support transactions, Google Forms to receive inquiries, and website hosting services to make this site available. These providers may process information needed to perform their services and may maintain their own privacy policies. Shipping providers receive delivery information when needed to fulfill an order. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Cookies and Technical Data

We do not intentionally deploy advertising or visitor-analytics tracking technologies. Our website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for essential operation, security, fraud prevention, and payment functionality. Hosting providers may automatically collect technical records such as IP addresses, browser information, and access times.

Email Updates

If you subscribe to email updates, we use the email address you provide to send information related to SIHYEON PARK. You may ask to stop receiving these messages at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We do not use purchase or inquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis.

Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, fulfill orders, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, or comply with law. Retention periods can differ by type of record and service provider. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information we hold, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may need to retain certain transaction or legal records. You can also choose not to submit optional information.

Children

Our website and products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Updates and Contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Changes will appear here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact sihyeonpark.art@gmail.com.
